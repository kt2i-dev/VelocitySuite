Problem

Problem statement *RM-1042 shafts from ABC Precision Components arrived 0.28 mm oversize (lot SUP-84721). A similar NCR on the same part in 03/2025 makes this a repeat.
ScopeRM-1042 and the other turned parts ABC Precision makes on lathe L-4 (RM-1043, RM-1050).
Business impact40 units shipped to a customer, 25 rejected, 6 hours of line downtime on Line 3.

Root cause

Category *Supplier · Method
Confirmed byMaria Lopez, 09/09/2026
Confirmed root cause *The supplier tracked cutting-tool life by hand, so tool wear on lathe L-4 went unnoticed and the diameter drifted oversize.
Escape point *Final inspection at the supplier sampled 1 part in 50, too few to catch the drift.

Corrective action progress

Actions4
Verified2
Awaiting verification1
In progress1

5 Whys

1Why were the shafts oversize? The cutting tool on lathe L-4 was worn.
2Why was the tool worn? It wasn’t changed at its rated life of 400 parts.
3Why wasn’t it changed? Tool life was tracked by hand on a paper log.
4Why by hand? Lathe L-4 has no tool-life counter.
5Why no counter? The supplier’s control plan doesn’t require one for turned parts.
