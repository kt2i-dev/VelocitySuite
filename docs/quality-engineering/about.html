About Quality / Engineering

A connected path through NCR, CAPA, SCAR and MRB.

Why quality

Quality is no longer just a final inspection step. It affects operational performance, customer satisfaction, regulatory compliance, brand reputation and profitability. Yet many manufacturers still manage quality events, nonconformances, investigations and corrective actions across disconnected legacy applications, spreadsheets, email and paper, which makes accountability, audit trails, spotting recurring issues and measuring corrective-action effectiveness hard.

The conversation

  • Trigger: corrective actions span spreadsheets and disconnected systems.
  • Workflow: record the issue, assign actions, capture evidence, track to closure.
  • Buyer: a quality leader working with engineering and operations.

Regulated or complex manufacturers feel this most acutely.

The first solution

Quality Event & Corrective Action Management manages quality events from identification through investigation, root-cause analysis, corrective and preventive action, effectiveness verification, approval and closure. It adapts to the manufacturer’s own methods: CAPA, NCR, 8D, 5-Why, Ishikawa / fishbone, containment and supplier corrective action.

What it can grow into

An extensible quality foundation for further Velocity Suite capabilities: Supplier Quality Management, Audit Management, Document & Change Control, Inspection Management, Engineering Change Management and predictive quality analytics, with one connected data and application architecture.
