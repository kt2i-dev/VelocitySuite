About Manufacturing

Where Velocity Suite helps manufacturers, and which applications come first.

The problem

Manufacturers run on an ERP and an MES, but plenty of work still happens between them: chasing a supplier for a late confirmation, tracking a corrective action across spreadsheets, finding out where a bad lot went. Those workflows are where these applications start.

Where we help

  • Supply chain and logistics: supplier coordination, parts availability and exception handling.
  • Quality and engineering: NCR, CAPA, SCAR and MRB workflows, with lot traceability.
  • Operations: production visibility and work execution across plant systems.

First applications

  • Quality Assurance (Quality Event and Corrective Action Management). Corrective actions spread across spreadsheets and disconnected systems become one path: record the issue, assign actions, capture evidence and track it to closure. Usually led by a quality leader working with engineering and operations. Quality Assurance
  • Supplier Portal. Late confirmations and missing supplier documents become shared requests, status, documents and exceptions. Usually led by a supply chain leader with IT sponsorship. (coming soon)
  • Spare Parts. (coming soon)

Possible later applications

Candidate applications by area. None of these are planned yet.

  • Supply chain and logistics: supplier scorecard, demand forecasting, procurement and onboarding, fabrication tracking, JIT integration, digital kanban, warehouse management.
  • Production and maintenance: shift handoff and follow-up actions, CMMS and asset tracking, factory performance (OEE), SQDCME and Gemba dashboards, 5S audits, SOP knowledge base, technician dispatch, factory scheduling and work orders, an operator portal.
  • Safety and environment: lockout/tagout, permit to work, OSHA compliance, hazard and risk assessment, incident reporting and audits, digital product passport, ESG and carbon tracking.
  • Quality and engineering: FMEA, PPAP, inspection audits, EBOM/MBOM, critical characteristics, new product management, scrap tracking and cost, kaizen and continuous improvement.
  • Workforce and training: on-the-job skills tracking, staff scheduling, learning and compliance training, temporary workforce management, time cards.
