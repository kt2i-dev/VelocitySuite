About Velocity Suite

KT2i’s Velocity Suite for Manufacturing: reusable starting points built on OutSystems, tailored to the customer.

What it is

The Velocity Manufacturing Suite is built around a simple idea: manufacturers should be able to modernize critical operational processes quickly, without being held back by legacy technology or long development cycles. Its applications complement a manufacturer’s existing ERP, MES, QMS, PLM, laboratory, supplier and shop-floor systems rather than replacing them.

The entry point is often a workflow stranded between core systems: where an ERP or MES leaves people coordinating work by email.

The approach

  • Integrate with ERP, MES and existing data sources.
  • Adapt screens, rules and approvals to the plant.
  • Expand by workflow as customer demand is proven.

The first starting points are a Supplier Portal, Spare Parts and Quality workflows.

What makes it different

1. Built on an application platform, not a fixed product

Workflows, business rules, forms, integrations, dashboards and AI capabilities can evolve with the business: new event types, changed approval routing, new plants, suppliers and data sources, new KPIs, without waiting for a vendor’s release.

2. Designed for manufacturing complexity

Records connect to the context around them (product, lot, supplier, work order, line, machine, shift, process parameters, maintenance history), so an issue is understood in full instead of as an isolated record.

3. Continuous improvement, not a one-time implementation

Administrators adjust workflows, classifications, rules and dashboards through configuration. When something goes beyond configuration, KT2i adds targeted OutSystems extensions without destabilizing the core.

AI built in from the ground up

AI augments people; it doesn’t replace their judgment. People remain responsible for decisions, approvals and dispositions. AI capabilities can be introduced in phases:

1 · AI assistance→2 · Investigation intelligence→3 · Predictive quality→4 · Agentic operations

The maturity path it supports:

Reactive→Controlled→Connected→Intelligent→Predictive

The five sectors

  • Quality / Engineering: NCR, CAPA, SCAR and MRB workflows with traceability; FMEA, PPAP, inspection, continuous improvement.
  • Supply Chain / Logistics: supplier coordination, parts availability, exception handling.
  • Production / Maintenance: shift handoff, maintenance and assets, factory performance, scheduling, the connected worker.
  • Safety / Environment: lockout/tagout, permits, OSHA compliance, incidents, ESG.
  • Workforce / Training: skills, scheduling, training, time cards.

How the sectors connect

Quality Event & Corrective Action Management is intended to become the quality orchestration layer of the suite, so one event reaches whichever application has to act on it:

  • Supplier management: supplier issue → SCAR → supplier scorecard
  • Incoming inspection: inspection failure → Quality Event → NCR
  • Manufacturing operations: production deviation → Quality Event
  • Maintenance: equipment root cause → maintenance work order
  • Inventory / warehouse: NCR → material hold
  • Customer service: customer complaint → Quality Event → CAPA
  • Engineering change: CAPA → engineering change request
  • Document management: CAPA → SOP / work-instruction revision
  • Training management: CAPA → retraining requirement

Systems it integrates with

ERP (materials, suppliers, purchase orders, receipts, work orders, inventory, customers, shipments), MES (production orders, operations, equipment, lot production and consumption, measurements), WMS (inventory, locations, lots, holds, movements), PLM (products, revisions, BOMs, specifications, engineering changes), LIMS, CMMS / EAM, supplier management, CRM and customer service, data platforms and IoT, identity (Microsoft Entra ID, Active Directory, Okta) and collaboration (Teams, Outlook, Slack). Velocity Suite owns the process; it doesn’t become the system of record for data those systems already hold.
